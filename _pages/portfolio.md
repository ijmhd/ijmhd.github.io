My group develops high-order numerical methods and high-performance simulation tools to understand how solar wind, planetary rotation, internal plasma sources, and ionospheric coupling shape space environments across the Solar System.

<div class="notice--info" markdown="1">
**Current programme:** RGC Research Fellow Scheme 2025/26, *Multi-scale Modeling and Forecasting of Planetary Space Environment* (PI; HK$5.47 million).
</div>

<div class="research-grid">
  <article class="research-card">
    <p class="research-kicker">Comparative planetology</p>
    <h2>Planetary space environments</h2>
    <p>Comparative modelling of Earth, Venus, Mars, Ganymede, Jupiter, and Saturn, with emphasis on solar-wind forcing, atmospheric escape, plasma transport, and mission-relevant space weather.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="research-card">
    <p class="research-kicker">Giant planets</p>
    <h2>Jupiter and Saturn</h2>
    <p>Global dynamics of rapidly rotating magnetospheres, including interchange instability, magnetotail reconnection, magnetopause structure, mass loading, and the drivers of auroral variability.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="research-card">
    <p class="research-kicker">Coupled systems</p>
    <h2>Aurora and magnetosphere-ionosphere coupling</h2>
    <p>Alfvénic energy transport, field-aligned currents, auroral streamers, cusp dynamics, ionospheric control, and a unified physical framework for planetary auroral morphologies.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="research-card">

    <h2>High-order MHD and GAMERA-OP</h2>
    <p>Geometry-aware finite-volume algorithms, divergence-free magnetic-field evolution, angular-momentum conservation, and extensible solvers for planetary and astrophysical plasma applications.</p>

## Selected recent outcomes

- **GAMERA-OP**, a three-dimensional finite-volume MHD solver for orthogonal curvilinear geometries, published in *The Astrophysical Journal Supplement Series* (2026).
- Shock-induced magnetic reconnection in the Venusian magnetotail, published in *Nature Communications* (2026).
- The role of planetary rotation in polar cusp localization, published in *Geophysical Research Letters* (2026).
- A unified framework for global auroral morphologies of different planets, published in *Nature Astronomy* (2024).
