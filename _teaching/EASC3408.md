Geophysics uses physical principles, observations, and quantitative models to investigate the Earth as an interconnected system. The course covered the solid Earth, oceans, atmosphere, ionosphere, and near-Earth space environment, while introducing applications in natural hazards, resources, environmental monitoring, and planetary exploration.

## Topics covered

- Earth's internal structure, gravity, and magnetic field
- Seismology and earthquakes
- Heat flow, mantle dynamics, and plate tectonics
- Applied geophysical methods
- Atmosphere, ionosphere, and near-Earth space
- Earth-Sun and Earth-Moon interactions
- Geophysical observations, data interpretation, and modelling
